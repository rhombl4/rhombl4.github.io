📲 Install for Offline Practice

DrumPad Buddy runs 100% offline without internet! Once installed, you can practice anywhere: practice rooms, basements, or on the go.

🌐 Google Chrome (Android & Mobile)
  1. Tap Chrome's three dots menu (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Open the DrumPad app from your home screen — no internet needed!
🍎 Chrome & Safari on iPhone / iPad
  1. Tap the Share button 📤 (in the address bar or bottom menu).
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" ➕.
  3. Launch it from your home screen for full-screen offline mode!
